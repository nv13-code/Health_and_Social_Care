Built from the Pearson BTEC Level 3 National Extended Diploma in Health and Social Care specification (Issue 10, June 2022). Default exam grade boundaries are from Pearson's June 2026 series. Boundaries change every series, so treat the points from raw marks as estimates. Your teacher's assessment decisions and Pearson's results are what count.

When you open this page signed in to claude.ai, your progress saves privately to your account and follows you between devices. Nobody else can see it, including the page's owner. A copy also stays in this browser. Backup in the Planner tab gives you a code you can keep as an extra copy.
