# Single-student pilot plan: HSC D* hub

**Length:** 6 weeks. **Participant:** one Year 12 or 13 Extended Diploma student, using their own Claude account. **Lead:** the HSC tutor.

## What the pilot has to show
| Question | Evidence | Success looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Does the student remember more over time? | Pilot summary → `recallByGap`: accuracy on reviews after 7, 14 and 30 days | At least 70% correct after 7 days and at least 60% after 14 days by week 6 |
| Is the student's confidence calibrated? | Pilot summary → `confidence` | When "certain", at least 85% correct; the gap between certain and guessing widens |
| Is the routine sustainable? | `daysActive`, `sessionRun`, `tasksTicked` | Active on at least 4 days a week for 5 of the 6 weeks |
| Do the walkthroughs lead to Distinction-ready evidence? | Evidence map plus the tutor's review of one assignment against the D criteria | Every D requirement is mapped "Yes" with a location the tutor agrees with |
| Is the work still authentic? | Tutor's authenticity check (below) | No passages traceable to hub examples, starters or AI |
| Can the student use it? | Think-aloud sessions in weeks 1 and 4 | No step where the student is stuck for more than 2 minutes without a recovery |

## Timeline
- **Week 0:** The student sets up their own copy using `student-quickstart.md` and enters dates. The tutor adds the college's DSL name to the help panel, or tells the student who it is.
- **Week 1:** Think-aloud session 1 (20 minutes).
- **Weeks 1–6:** Daily quiz, weekly tasks, walkthroughs for the current assignment.
- **Week 3:** The student shares a pilot summary (Planner → Copy pilot summary), if they're willing.
- **Week 4:** Think-aloud session 2.
- **Week 6:** Final pilot summary, the tutor's review of the assignment, and a 10-minute interview.

## Think-aloud protocol (20 minutes)
1. Ask the student to say aloud everything they're thinking as they work. Don't help unless they're stuck for 2 minutes.
2. Tasks: (a) finish today's quiz; (b) open the Distinction coach and do one walkthrough step for their current unit; (c) find next week's tasks; (d) find where to get help.
3. Record: where they hesitate, misread or skip, any words they don't know, and what they say about the confidence rating.
4. Close: "What was most useful? What would you change? Did anything feel like it was doing the work for you?"

## Tutor authenticity check (per assignment)
- [ ] Compare the work with the parallel-case examples and sentence starters for that unit (Distinction coach). Check for no shared phrasing beyond common subject terms.
- [ ] The student can explain any paragraph you point at, without notes.
- [ ] Case details, results and quotes match the brief or a referenced source.
- [ ] Any AI use is acknowledged as your centre's policy requires.
- [ ] Assessment decision recorded; no post-decision coaching given (Pearson rules).

## Data and safeguarding
- Progress is stored in the student's own Claude account. The tutor sees only what the student chooses to share. The pilot summary holds numbers only and never their writing.
- The hub covers sensitive topics and has content notes and a help panel. Make sure the student knows who the college's DSL is.
- Under-18 student: get your centre's agreement for the pilot. If any data is collected beyond what the student shares voluntarily, check with your data protection lead.

## Decision at week 6
- **Scale to a class** if most of the success measures are met. Accounts would then move to SciencePass, which needs a DPIA.
- **Revise and re-pilot** if usability or authenticity issues show up.
- **Stop** if there's no gain in recall or calibration after 6 weeks of regular use.
