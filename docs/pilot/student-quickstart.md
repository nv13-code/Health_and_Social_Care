# HSC D* hub: getting started

## Set up (10 minutes, once)
1. In your own Claude account, start a new chat and attach `hsc-dstar-v2.html`. Your tutor will give it to you, or you can download it from `dist/` in the GitHub repo.
2. Paste this: *"Publish this HTML file as an artifact exactly as it is, with the capabilities db, user and downloads enabled, so my progress saves to my account and I can save backup files."*
3. Open the artifact. The pill at the top right should change to **Saved to your account** after your first click.
4. **Points tab:** choose your pathway and your five optional units.
5. **Planner tab:** add your exam dates and every assignment's launch and hand-in dates from your college's assessment plan.

## Every day (about 15 minutes)
**Today tab:** answer your daily questions. Before you see the options, say how sure you are, and be honest. That's how the hub finds the topics you *think* you know but don't.

## Every week
- Tick off **This week** on the Today tab.
- Write one timed extended answer (**Exam revision → Extended answers**), then reveal the marking points and tick only what you actually wrote.
- On Sunday, re-rate your topic checklists.

## When you're working on an assignment
1. **Distinction coach** → your unit → read every D criterion.
2. Work through each **Guided walkthrough**. The examples use a made-up case on purpose. Learn the thinking, then write about your own brief in your own words.
3. Before you hand in, complete the **evidence map**: every requirement should say *Yes*, with a place in your work you could show your tutor.

## Your privacy
The hub never asks your name and doesn't track you. What you type isn't saved: it disappears when you close the page, unless you turn on **Save my writing** in Planner → Your privacy. Your ticks and quiz progress are saved privately for you only. **Delete everything** removes them.

**Back up once a week:** Planner → Backup → **Save backup file**. Tick **Include my writing** if you want to keep practice answers. Keep the file somewhere safe, such as your college drive. **Restore from file** brings everything back on any device.

## Keep it honest
The hub never writes your assignment, and nothing you type in it is read by anyone. Don't paste its examples, or anything an AI writes, into your work. You sign a declaration that the work is yours.

## Need help?
The **Need to talk to someone?** section at the bottom of every page lists who you can contact.
